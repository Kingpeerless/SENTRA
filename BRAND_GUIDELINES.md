# SENTRA — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Foundation

**Product:** SENTRA — AI Agent Security Infrastructure  
**Tagline:** AI can act. SENTRA keeps it accountable.  
**Positioning:** Trust is not assumed. It is enforced.

SENTRA should feel like premium enterprise cybersecurity infrastructure: minimal, sophisticated, credible, and technical.

## Typography

- **Interface text:** Inter or Manrope
- **Technical data:** JetBrains Mono

Use interface typography for readable product experiences and monospace typography for technical data.

## Logo Direction

Use an abstract geometric shield with a subtle central control point. The mark should communicate governed control and security without relying on familiar, generic cybersecurity tropes.

Avoid:

- Robot heads
- Generic shield imagery
- Coin icons

## Voice and Product Communication

Communicate with clarity and confidence. Emphasize enforceable controls, deterministic decisions, and accountability rather than assumed trust.

Clearly label whether a feature is implemented or planned. Keep simulations visibly distinct from real blockchain transactions, and make **ALLOW**, **WARN**, and **BLOCK** decisions unambiguous. A deterministic **BLOCK** must never be presented as overridable by AI.
